BARVINOK

A Ukrainian culture club in the Netherlands bringing children and families together through singing, creative activities and community events.

01

About the club

BARVINOK creates a place for Ukrainian children and families in the Netherlands to meet, sing, create and stay connected to their culture.

02

OTUT and BARVINOK

BARVINOK is an independent club with its own name and community. OTUT helps with its development and communications.
