Social project

Rhythms of Life

A social project by Anastasiia Semenchenko. Music and rhythm are ways to connect, participate and act together.

01

Archive and origins

Rhythms of Life was started by Anastasiia Semenchenko in Ukraine in 2008. From 2009 to 2012, it brought people together through gatherings, workshops and events, with music as a social element. Part of that history is preserved in archive material.

What is happening now

The project is developing internationally with OTUT support. Partner and research conversations are under way; no pilot agreement, budget or funding is confirmed.
