EMAP residencies
for media artists.

The European Media Art Platform is accepting applications for two-month production residencies in 2027 from artists, duos and collectives working in digital, media, robotic and bio art.

Every proposal must be collaborative: apply as a duo or collective, with a chosen partner, or with the intention of finding a local collaborator through the host institution. Artists resident or paying taxes in Ukraine are eligible.

DURATION2 MONTHS IN 2027
NETWORK15 HOSTS ACROSS EUROPE
DEADLINE6 NOVEMBER · 14:00 CET

What the programme covers

  • €4,000 grant for the main applicant;
  • €2,000 for the collaborating artist;
  • a separate €4,000 production budget;
  • accommodation, travel within EU ceilings, and access to studios and media labs.

Conditions checked on 30 September 2026. Always read the full official call and FAQ before applying.
