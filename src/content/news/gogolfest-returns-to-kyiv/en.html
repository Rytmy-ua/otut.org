16.09.2026FESTIVAL NEWS

GOGOLFEST returns
to Kyiv.

The anniversary edition “20 Years Later” opens today, 16 September, and runs through 20 September at the International Centre of Culture and Arts.

Following changes made for safety, the 2026 programme is concentrated in the Kinopalats and the adjacent Backyard. Five days bring together film, acoustic music, visual art, immersive performance, education and public conversations in a smaller, flexible format designed to avoid large crowds.

OPENING16 SEPTEMBER · 16:00
OPEN TOUR BY VLAD TROITSKYI
LOCATIONKINOPALATS + BACKYARD
ICCA, KYIV
ALREADY HAVE A TICKET?

Previously purchased passes and tickets for individual events remain valid and give access to the festival on any day from 16 to 20 September, for the full festival period. If you keep a ticket for a major event postponed to May 2027, you will automatically receive a full pass for the large-scale GOGOLFEST in 2027.

The programme may change in response to the security situation. Check the official website before visiting and follow the festival team’s safety instructions.
