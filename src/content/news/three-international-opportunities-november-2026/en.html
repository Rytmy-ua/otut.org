17.10.2026 · OPEN CALLS

Three international opportunities for artists and cultural professionals

Three calls with early November deadlines: a media art residency, festival partnerships and residencies for Ukrainian artists in Finland.

01

EU

EMAP Residencies 2027

Who it is for: Artists working in digital, media, robotic or bio art. The proposal must be collaborative: a duo, collective or project with another artist.

What is offered: Two-month production residency; €4,000 artist grant, €2,000 for a collaborating artist and €4,000 production budget. Accommodation and travel support are provided under the programme rules.

Deadline: 2026-11-06 · 14:00 CET

Official call ↗
02

EU

EFFEA Call #5

Who it is for: Emerging artists or collectives supported by a partnership of at least three festivals in three eligible countries. The lead festival submits the application; artists cannot apply directly.

What is offered: 40 Discovery grants of €8,000 and 10 Springboard grants of €15,000 for festival partnerships and artistic development.

Deadline: 2026-11-03

Official call ↗
03

FI

Ukraine Solidarity Residencies 2027

Who it is for: Ukrainian and Ukraine-based artists and arts practitioners affected by the war. Applications are in English; written, audio and video responses are possible.

What is offered: Residencies in Finland include a place to live and work, one return trip and a €1,200 monthly working grant, subject to the official conditions.

Deadline: 2026-11-02 · 15:00 GMT+2

Official call ↗

Check eligibility, partner requirements and the full terms on each organiser’s site before applying. Selection and funding are not guaranteed.

Interested in one of these calls? Write to OTUT at info@otut.org. We can help you understand the requirements and plan your next steps.
