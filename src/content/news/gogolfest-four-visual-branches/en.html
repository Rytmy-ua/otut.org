30.09.2026AFTER THE FESTIVAL

Four branches.
One space.

At the anniversary Kyiv edition of GOGOLFEST, the visual programme worked not as a single exhibition but as a meeting of four distinct artistic environments.

01ART POWER
02INSTYTUT AVTOMATYKY
03HERE AND NOW
04PREMONITION OF CHANGE

The festival brought artists, collectives and curatorial groups together at the ICCA Kinopalats. Film, acoustic music, immersive performance, education and public conversations unfolded alongside them — intimate in scale, without losing the multidisciplinary character with which GOGOLFEST began in Kyiv in 2007.

Not one shared exhibition, but four different ways of speaking about the present inside one festival space.
