GOGOLFEST has published the full AntiDot education programme within Pokrova: TimeCode, running in Zaporizhzhia from 2 to 4 October.

Three days of conversations about the city, trust, veteran reintegration, communities, local memory and Zaporizhzhia’s future, with curators, researchers and practitioners from different fields.

CURRENT · CHECKED 01.10.2026

Advance registration is required. Places are limited; check current availability on GOGOLFEST’s official channels.

Full AntiDot education programme

02.10City

12:00Local History: Zaporizhzhia

Presentation of a special issue and a short conversation about the Zaporizhzhia revealed through it.

13:30The city that does not stop

A panel on what Zaporizhzhia has invented during the war and where the boundary of the possible lies.

03.10People and communities

11:00How do we build trust, unity and dialogue?

A lecture on trust and dialogue in a community. Speaker: Vlad Troitskyi.

12:45Veteran reintegration

A panel on veterans’ return and why it is already a shared social question.

14:10The community that does not wait

A practical conversation about working with communities in Ukraine’s South and East during the war. Speaker: Valeriia Ovcharova, YES NGO.

15:00Garage Hub: The city sounds

A music laboratory for internally displaced people and local residents.

04.10Future. Zaporizhzhia 2030

12:00The Zaporizhzhia we do not know

A non-academic lecture: 10–15 lesser-known stories, places and people changing how the city is seen. Speaker: Denys Vasyliev.

13:00Zaporizhzhia after

A panel on what past we take with us into the future.

14:30We live while there is a chance

Open lecture. Speaker: Maksym Demskyi.
