OTUT services — from idea to realisation

Have a cultural idea? Let’s bring it to life.

A performance, workshop, community event or international collaboration needs practical steps: a clear proposal, partners, a budget and coordination.

OTUT helps artists, creative teams and organisers

  • Shape an idea into a project: define its purpose, audience, format and work plan.
  • Find relevant opportunities: festivals, residencies, open calls and potential partners.
  • Prepare materials: presentations, partnership proposals, applications and budgets.
  • Coordinate collaboration: partner communication, agreeing terms and preparing events.

You can bring us a specific task or discuss support for a whole project. We agree the scope, timeline and fee before work begins.

Email info@otut.org with a few sentences about what you want to create, what you already have and where you need help. We’ll suggest where to start.
